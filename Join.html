⏱  Join times from November to March;

  • UTC – 9.00am
  • Brisbane = 7.00pm - AEST (Australian Eastern Standard Time)
  • Sydney / Melbourne = 8.00pm - AEDT (Australian Eastern Daylight-Saving Time)
  • Paris = 10.00am - CET (Central European Time)
  • London = 9.00am - GMT (Greenwich Mean Time)

⏱  Join times from April to October;

  • UTC – 9.30am
  • Brisbane / Sydney / Melbourne = 7.30pm - AEST (Australian Eastern Standard Time)
  • Paris = 11.30am - CEST (Central European Summer Time)
  • London = 10.30am - BST (British Summer Time)

How to join our online flights

  • Open Condor3 and check what version of you have. Then close..

  • Visit either of the following Serverlists

Condor Serverlist

Hitziger Alternate Serverlist
  • Find   AUS VirtualGliding   in the Serverlist
  • Your condor3 version has to be the same as what’s listed.
  • Check Status is on Joining Enabled.
  • Check you have the listed landscape, installed.
  • Click JOIN button.
  • Condor3 will start to Multiplayer window where click join again.
  • The server join time is usually about 20mins.
  • Once join time has finished you can only join as a spectator.
